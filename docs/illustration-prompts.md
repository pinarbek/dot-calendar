# Desk Life Calendar — prompts

Every prompt = STYLE (always the same) + SCENE (changes each month) + FORMAT (always the same).

Recurring characters:
- **Her:** a young woman with a short black bob haircut and round glasses.
- **Him:** a young man with messy wavy chestnut-brown hair, light stubble and thin rectangular glasses.
- **The cat:** a chubby orange cat that appears every month and is always completely indifferent (a running joke).

---

## STYLE (fixed)

Original hand-drawn digital illustration in a charming Japanese slice-of-life anime background-art style, matching the visual language of a warm, everyday-life anime film. Thin, slightly wobbly organic lineart, drawn in darker shades of each object's local color rather than black. Flat cel colors, minimal and soft shading, subtle paper grain texture, gentle ambient light and natural imperfections. Muted, desaturated pastel palette dominated by dusty brown, warm cream, faded sage green, soft terracotta and powder blue. Natural anime proportions, expressive but understated facial expressions. Rich, cozy, lived-in environment filled with small everyday objects and visual storytelling. Slightly elevated three-quarter perspective, intimate composition, warm nostalgic atmosphere.

## FORMAT (fixed)

The entire scene should feel like a funny but relatable everyday moment, visually rich, charming and slightly absurd. Add charming, subtle visual jokes through the objects and their arrangement, not through exaggerated cartoon effects. Arrange the illustration as one compact, cohesive vignette, centered on a pure white background with generous clean white space surrounding it, suitable for a calendar illustration. No text, no typography, no logo, no watermark, no signature. Vertical 3:4 composition.

---

## SCENES

**JANUARY (her)** — the scene you wrote (New Year's resolutions, the computer is freezing).

**FEBRUARY (him) — Valentine's panic.** The young man sits at his desk late in the evening, frantically typing a love letter on his laptop, surrounded by a small mountain of crumpled pink paper hearts from failed drafts. He wears a cozy knitted sweater with a single tiny heart on it, and his face shows the deep concentration of someone choosing between "Dear" and "Hey". A heart-shaped box of chocolates is already half eaten, a single red rose stands in a coffee mug, there are sticky notes with crossed-out rhymes, and a dictionary is open to the letter L. The orange cat sits on the stack of discarded hearts like a throne, wearing one heart sticker on its forehead and looking unimpressed. Accent: soft dusty rose.

**MARCH (her) — spring arrives indoors.** She works at her desk by an open window on the first warm day. Spring has arrived a little too enthusiastically: small flowers and green sprouts are growing out between the keys of her keyboard, and a vine is curling around her monitor stand. She is sneezing mid-type, holding a tissue, eyes watering, while a little sparrow sits on her monitor. Desk details: a watering can next to the coffee mug, a seed packet, a pollen allergy spray, a vase of tulips, a light spring jacket on the chair. The orange cat sleeps in a flower pot that has clearly been taken over. Accent: fresh pale green and soft yellow.

**APRIL (him) — April showers.** A leaky ceiling above his desk on a rainy day. He keeps working calmly on his laptop under an open umbrella clipped to his chair, wearing a yellow raincoat over pajamas. Pots, mugs and a small bucket collect drips around the desk, one of them holding a tiny rubber duck. Rain streaks the window behind him and there are a few painted Easter eggs in a bowl. Rain boots stand under the desk and a weather app on the screen shows only rain clouds. The orange cat is perfectly dry and asleep in a cardboard box lined with a towel. Accent: rainy powder blue and grey-green.

**MAY (her) — desk garden.** Her desk has fully become an indoor garden: hanging plants, herbs in jars, a tomato seedling and a little terrarium. She works on a balcony doorway with her laptop half hidden among leaves, wearing a sun hat and gardening gloves, one glove tapping the keyboard. A butterfly has landed on her glasses and she is staying very still so as not to scare it. Iced coffee, pruning scissors next to the mouse, a plant-care book, a small fan. The orange cat is chewing a leaf of the most expensive plant. Accent: lush sage and leaf green.

**JUNE (him) — summer begins.** The first hot day. He works at a desk set up next to an inflatable kiddie pool, his feet in the water, wearing sunglasses pushed up on his head, a floral short-sleeve shirt and shorts. An ice cream cone in his free hand is melting onto a napkin as he tries to type fast. On the desk: a laptop with a towel draped over the back, a big glass of lemonade with a straw, sunscreen, an inflatable flamingo leaning against the chair, a beach ball. The orange cat lies flat on the cool tile floor like a pancake. Accent: sunny lemon yellow and pool aqua.

**JULY (her) — heatwave.** Peak summer heat. She has given up on sitting: she is lying on the floor in front of a big electric fan with her laptop on her stomach, her bob hair blowing straight back, eyes closed, wearing a loose tank top and shorts with a cold towel on her forehead. Her feet are in a bucket of ice water. Around her: a half-eaten watermelon, popsicle sticks, a stack of unopened paperwork used as a fan, a thermometer stuck at the top. The orange cat is sitting directly in front of the fan, stealing all the air. Accent: watermelon pink and warm cream.

**AUGUST (him) — out of office.** He is "on vacation" but clearly still working: lying in a hammock strung up inside his room, laptop balanced on his chest, wearing a bucket hat and a Hawaiian shirt, with an inflatable neck pillow. A suitcase is half packed and overflowing on the floor, a paper "out of office" sign is taped to the monitor behind him (with no readable text), a coconut drink with an umbrella sits on a stool, and travel brochures are scattered around. The orange cat is sleeping inside the open suitcase on top of the clothes. Accent: tropical turquoise and sand.

**SEPTEMBER (her) — back to work.** First week after summer. She sits behind a desk piled high with paperwork, folders and an overflowing inbox tray, peeking over the top of the stack with a determined but tired face, wearing a cardigan and a still-sunburned nose. A tower of empty coffee cups, a fresh new planner, a pencil case, a red apple, and a framed vacation photo turned face-down. The first yellow autumn leaves blow in through the window. The orange cat is sleeping on the keyboard, making it impossible to work. Accent: mustard yellow and warm brown.

**OCTOBER (him) — Halloween overtime.** A spooky late evening. He keeps working on his laptop while wearing a carved jack-o'-lantern on his head like a helmet, eyes peeking through the carved holes, sitting calmly in an oversized hoodie. A friendly little ghost peeks out from behind the monitor, a fake spider web stretches across the desk lamp, candy wrappers are scattered everywhere, and a bowl of candy is labeled for trick-or-treaters but is clearly already half eaten by him. Small pumpkins, autumn leaves, a pumpkin spice latte and a witch's broom leaning against the chair. The orange cat wears a tiny witch hat and looks deeply offended. Accent: pumpkin orange and deep plum.

**NOVEMBER (her) — cozy autumn mode.** A grey rainy afternoon. She sits in a big armchair with a laptop on a knitting project on her lap, half working and half knitting a scarf that is ridiculously long and loops across the whole room. A pot of tea with a knitted cozy, a stack of books, a scented candle, roasted chestnuts on a plate, rain on the window, fallen leaves on the windowsill. The orange cat is tangled up in the yarn ball and has given up trying to escape. Accent: rust red and warm chestnut.

**DECEMBER (him) — tangled in the holidays.** Christmas week. He sits at his desk in a Santa hat, completely tangled in a long string of fairy lights that are wrapped around him, his chair and his monitor, still trying to answer one last email before the holidays. A small decorated Christmas tree is leaning dangerously, gifts are half wrapped with tape stuck to his fingers, a plate of gingerbread cookies with one bitten off, a mug of mulled wine, a red sweater with a reindeer. Snow falls outside the window. The orange cat is lying in the empty gift box instead of the gift. Accent: deep cranberry red and pine green.
